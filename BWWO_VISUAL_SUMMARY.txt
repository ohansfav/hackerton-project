🌊 THE 4 PHASES

Phase 1: PROPAGATION
  Particles move toward best solution (exploitation)
  
Phase 2: REFRACTION
  Escape local optima when stuck (exploration)
  
Phase 3: BREAKING
  Create new random solutions (diversity)
  
Phase 4: REFLECTION
  Ensure solutions are valid (constraints)
